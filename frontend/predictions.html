How Our AI Model Predicts UV Risk

The planned ML model learns relationships between environmental features: total column ozone, cloud cover, solar zenith angle and radiation, temperature, humidity and aerosols. It outputs an hourly UV Index with an uncertainty range. Forecasts become less certain further ahead, and uncertainty bands show a plausible range rather than guaranteed accuracy.
