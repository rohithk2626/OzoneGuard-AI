The basics

One atmosphere. Several connected signals.

Ozone, clouds, sunlight and local weather all play a part in the UV conditions we experience.

Ozone is a natural shield

High in the stratosphere, ozone absorbs much of the Sun’s harmful ultraviolet radiation before it reaches Earth’s surface.

UV changes through the day

Solar angle, season, latitude, altitude, clouds and atmospheric conditions can all influence UV intensity.

Weather adds context

Clouds and other local conditions affect exposure, but a cool or overcast day does not necessarily mean UV is low.

Everyday protection

Sun safety starts with awareness.

High UV exposure can affect skin and eyes. When UV is elevated, shade, protective clothing, a wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses and broad-spectrum sunscreen can help reduce exposure.
